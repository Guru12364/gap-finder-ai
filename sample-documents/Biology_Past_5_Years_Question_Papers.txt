REPEATED EXAM QUESTIONS ANALYSIS (LAST 5 YEARS):
- Question Type A (Frequently Missed): "Explain the chemiosmotic hypothesis of ATP generation in chloroplasts with a diagram showing proton movement."
  Common Mistake: 68% of students mistakenly draw protons accumulating in the stroma instead of the thylakoid lumen.
- Question Type B: "State the equation for photolysis of water. Name the mineral ions essential for this reaction."
  Correct Answer: 2H2O -> 4H+ + 4e- + O2. Essential ions: Manganese (Mn2+) and Chlorine (Cl-).
- Question Type C: "How many molecules of ATP and NADPH are required to fix 6 molecules of CO2 in the Calvin cycle?"
  Correct Answer: 18 ATP and 12 NADPH.
- Question Type D: "Explain why transpiration is called a necessary evil (Curtis, 1926)."
  Correct Answer: Water loss is inevitable as stomata must remain open for CO2 intake necessary for photosynthesis.
